What a fax cover sheet is and what it needs

A fax cover sheet is the first page of a fax. It tells the person at the other end who the fax is from, who it is for, the date, and how many pages to expect, so the pages reach the right desk. This generator makes one in your browser. It is free, needs no account, and uploads nothing.

  • To and From: names, plus a fax or phone number for each side.
  • Pages: count the cover sheet itself, so the recipient can tell if anything is missing.
  • Subject: a generic label such as "Records request" or "Signed form". Leave sensitive details out.
  • Confidentiality notice: a short standard line is common for business and medical faxes. It is not legal advice, so use your organization's wording if it has one.

How to make a fax cover sheet

  1. Enter the recipient's name and fax number under To.
  2. Enter your name and phone number under From.
  3. Set the date and the total page count, including the cover sheet.
  4. Add a short subject and, if needed, a one or two line message. Tick any flags such as Urgent or For review.
  5. Click Print or save as PDF. Choose Save as PDF in the print dialog if you plan to send it online.

Questions

What should a fax cover sheet include?

A fax cover sheet should show who the fax is from and who it is for, each with a name and phone or fax number, plus the date, the total number of pages including the cover sheet, and a short subject line. A confidentiality notice is common for business and medical faxes.

Is this fax cover sheet generator free?

Yes. It is free, and you do not need an account. Fill in the fields, then print the sheet or save it as a PDF from your browser.

Is the information I type sent anywhere?

No. The cover sheet is built in your browser with JavaScript. Nothing you type is uploaded, stored, or logged by this page.

Should I put private details on a fax cover sheet?

No. Anyone who handles the fax can read the cover sheet, so keep it to names, numbers, the date, and a generic subject. Put sensitive details such as diagnoses or account numbers inside the document itself.

Do I need a cover sheet when I send a fax online?
